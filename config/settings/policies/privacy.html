Hikmah Online is run by Hikmah Institute ("Hikmah", "we"). This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use hikmahonline.com, why we use it, who can see it and how long we keep it.

1. What we collect

  • Account: name, email address, username and password (never stored in readable form). If you sign in with Google, your Google name and email.
  • Profile: gender, date of birth, mobile / WhatsApp number, and a parent or guardian's contact for learners under 18.
  • Study records: enrolments, work, grades, live class attendance and certificates.
  • Payments: for paid courses, your transaction details and receipt.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and sign-in times, to keep the site secure.
  • Usage data: which pages you visit and what you click, and recordings of how pages are used, to see what works and fix what does not. Anything you type is hidden in these recordings. If you are signed in, this is linked to your account number only, never your name or email.

We use this data only to run your courses, check payments, record attendance, issue certificates and improve the site. We do not sell it and show no advertising. The site uses cookies to keep you signed in, and analytics cookies from PostHog (a service based in the United States) to collect the usage data above.

2. Who can see your data

  • Your teachers see your profile, work, grades and attendance in the courses they teach.
  • Other learners cannot see your profile or message you. In a shared class they do see your name, your posts, and your video, voice and chat in live classes. You can message your teachers, and they can message you.
  • Hikmah finance reviewers and site managers see your payment details and receipts. No one else can open a receipt, and it has no public link.
  • Hikmah administrators and support staff can see account data to run the site and help you.
  • Anyone with a certificate's code can verify it. Verification shows only your name, the course and the issue date.

4. How we protect it

The whole site uses HTTPS, passwords are never stored in readable form, and every uploaded file is scanned for viruses.

5. Your choices and rights

From your profile page you can:

  • see and correct your details;
  • download a copy of your data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • turn news and offers emails on or off.

We may keep some records after a deletion request where we need them for payments, certificates or a legal duty. For anything else, email hikmahonline1@gmail.com.

6. Children

Learners under 18 may use Hikmah Online only with a parent or guardian's permission. For them we ask for a parent or guardian's contact details.

7. Changes

When this policy changes, we'll ask you to read and accept the new version the next time you sign in.
