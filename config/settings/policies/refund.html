1. Before your payment is approved

If a Hikmah reviewer rejects your payment, nothing has been charged by us: you can resubmit a correct receipt or ask for your transfer back at hikmahonline1@gmail.com.

2. Cancelling a course you paid for

When you askRefund
Before the course startsFull fee, minus any bank or wallet transfer charges
Within 7 days after the first class, and you attended no more than 2 classes50% of the fee
Later than thatNo refund

3. If Hikmah cancels or moves a course

If we cancel a course, you get a full refund or a place on another course, as you choose. If we move it to new dates you can't attend, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of being told.

4. No refund

  • Free courses and free sessions.
  • Accounts closed for breaking the Terms of Use, including false or altered receipts.
  • Not attending classes, without asking for a refund in time.

5. How to ask and how you're paid

Email hikmahonline1@gmail.com with your name, the course and your payment reference. We reply within 5 working days. Approved refunds are sent back to the same bank account or wallet you paid from, within 14 working days. Your access to the course ends when the refund is made.
